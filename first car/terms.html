  4. Bears all costs of car repairs if any breakage is found during the rental or detention. Also bears all costs and rental charges during repair or detention and considering period of contract extended automatically until the car is discharged under the supervision of the renting company.
  5. The rental company has the right to add charges for additional milage and reserves the right according to this contract.
  6. If the milage counter is corrected the rental company has the right to force a penalty as it sees fit.

  13. The renter is responsible for paying an excess amount of Dhs 1500 and 20% as the insurance company sees fit in case of accident or damage.

  17. The contract shall be in force from the date of signing. He also must pay the amount for the expected rental period although he returned back the vehicle before the ending date.

  19. The rental company is not responsible about the security of any document regarding the renter (e.g. passports) after one week of delivering the car and closing the agreement unless he paid all his debts during the mentioned period.
